Coworker 1.0.0 for Windows

  • Hold Ctrl+Shift and talk: your words are typed into the app you are in.
  • Your dictionary, playbooks and voice shortcuts from your Coworker account.
  • A floating bar and a tray menu to switch model and language.
  • History, Analytics and Settings pages.
  • Updates install by themselves in the background, never in the middle of a dictation.
